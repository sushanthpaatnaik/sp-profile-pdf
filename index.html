Origin 03 / 16
An innovation journey that evolved from empathy-driven design into applied deep-tech systems.

Born in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, Sushanth was recognised by the Government of India as a six-time Presidential Awardee between 2008 and 2013 — among the youngest innovators to receive that honour multiple times.

His work began with a paralysed elderly man unable to communicate. That encounter became a breath-powered wheelchair — and from there, a career devoted to engineering empathy at the level of materials and systems.

He holds a BSc from IISER Bhopal (KVPY-SP) and a BEd from OCT, Bhopal. Today he leads Monoatom Labs, Grafillium, SPI Industries, InThinks, and Starunico Capital — while serving as CIO at Magppie.

He was among the youngest speakers invited to TED-India, a TEDx speaker on multiple occasions, and a fellow of MIT, INK, and NASA programmes.
