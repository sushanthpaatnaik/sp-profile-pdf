Menu 01 / About The Journey

An innovation journey that evolved from empathy-driven design into applied deep-tech systems.

Born in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, and recognized by the Government of India as a six-time Indian Presidential awardee between 2008-2013, Sushanth was among the youngest speakers invited to TED-India.

His work began with a paralyzed elderly man unable to communicate. That encounter became a breath-powered wheelchair—and from there, a career devoted to engineering empathy at the level of materials and systems.

He holds a BSc. from IISER Bhopal (KVPY-SP) and a BEd. in ETE from OCT, Bhopal. Today he leads Monoatom Labs, Grafillium, SPI Industries, InThinks, and Starunico Capital, while serving as CIO at Magppie.
